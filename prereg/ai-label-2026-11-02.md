# Pre-registration: do AI shopping agents discount "Made with AI" reviews the way people discount AI-labelled content?

Written 2 Nov 2026, before any data collection. The SHA-256 of this file is logged in `prereg/ai-label-2026-11-02.sha256`. It is not yet on OSF; upload it there unchanged (the user must do this from their own account).

## Human benchmark (direction only)
Carney, Riveros & Tully (2026, JCR, doi:10.1093/jcr/ucag013): AI-generated-content disclosures **reduce** consumer engagement (TikTok field data plus 8 preregistered experiments). The drop works through lower parasocial connection, driven partly by the creator's perceived effort. **Disclosures that signal greater effort reduce the drop.**
Limits of the comparison: their setting is creators on social media, and here it is product reviews. Their outcome is engagement, and here it is helpfulness and choice. Only the *direction* of effects is compared.

## Design
- **Items:** 100 real Amazon Reviews'23 reviews (4–5 stars, 30–150 words, "neutral" tier from the 1 Nov flagging, so no green claims). There are 25 each for shampoo, hand soap, laundry detergent and toothbrush. Seed 11.
- **Label conditions (within item):**
  - `none`: no label
  - `ai`: `[Label: Made with AI]`
  - `effort`: `[Label: Written by the reviewer after 3 months of use; AI helped with wording]`
- **Models:** qwen2.5:7b and llama3.1:8b (local, Ollama), temperature 0, seed 1, the same prompt templates as the 1 Nov pilot (`agent_run.py`).

**Outcome 1, helpfulness:** the agent rates the target review alone, shown with its label, from 1 to 7 (300 ratings per model).

**Outcome 2, choice:**
- Product A shows 4 neutral base reviews plus the target review in slot 3, carrying its label condition. Product B shows 4 different neutral base reviews.
- Both show a 4.5-star average and a $9.99 price.
- Both display orders are used, with the plain persona only.
- That gives 100 × 3 × 2 = 600 choices per model.

**Calibration:** the 40 dominance tasks from the pilot, in both orders. A model is excluded if it is below 95% correct.

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (human-like discount):** helpfulness(`ai`) < helpfulness(`none`).
- **H2 (effort mitigates):** helpfulness(`effort`) > helpfulness(`ai`).
- **H3:** P(choose A | `ai`) < P(choose A | `none`).
- **H4:** P(choose A | `effort`) > P(choose A | `ai`).

## Analysis
- **Helpfulness:** OLS of rating on condition dummies with item fixed effects; standard errors clustered by item.
- **Choice:** a linear probability model with item and order fixed effects; standard errors clustered by item.
- Tests are two-sided, α = .05. There are 4 hypotheses per model; also report Holm-adjusted p-values.
- Unparseable answers are excluded and their count is reported.
- Models are analysed separately. A finding counts as "robust" only if it has the same sign and is significant in both models.

## Decision rule (from file 98)
- **Go for M1:** at least one model shows a stable label effect (CI excluding 0), *and* it differs from the human pattern (opposite sign, or no effort mitigation) or differs between models.
- **No-go signal:** both models show no label effect in either outcome, or both exactly mirror the human pattern. Then there is no human–machine gap to theorise.

## Changes from the plan in file 98
- Temperature 0 instead of 0.7 × 20 runs, to stay comparable with the 1 Nov pilot. Variance comes from the 100 items.
- An `effort` label was added to test the paper's mitigation finding.
